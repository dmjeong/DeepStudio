LibreMobileNetV4 weights
------------------------

This product contains weights derived from timm (PyTorch Image Models)
(https://github.com/huggingface/pytorch-image-models).

Weights: mobilenetv4_conv_medium.e500_r224_in1k (ImageNet-1k, trained by Ross Wightman in timm;
no distillation, no extra data). Learned parameters are unchanged.
